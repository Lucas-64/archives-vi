# Archives VI Project Vision

## Vision

Archives VI aims to become the central community archive for Grand Theft Auto VI
mysteries, theories, easter eggs and collaborative investigations.

The product should feel like a digital investigation board: focused,
atmospheric and useful for players who want to document discoveries, compare
evidence and follow unresolved theories over time.

## Mission

Build a high-quality web platform where the GTA VI community can:

- Discover curated mysteries and cases.
- Track evidence, screenshots, videos and theory progress.
- Separate confirmed information from speculation.
- Collaborate around investigations in a structured way.
- Preserve discoveries as the game evolves after launch.

## Target Audience

- GTA VI fans interested in mysteries, easter eggs and hidden details.
- Content creators researching theories and discoveries.
- Community investigators who enjoy organizing evidence.
- Players who want a trusted archive instead of scattered posts.
- Portfolio reviewers evaluating product thinking, UI engineering and Scrum
  documentation.

## Long-Term Goals

- Provide a searchable archive of cases, theories and evidence.
- Support community contributions with moderation and review flows.
- Build an interactive Leonida map for location-based discoveries.
- Add authentication, profiles and contribution history.
- Establish a design system that can scale into a larger application.
- Prepare the codebase for a future Next.js migration after the Home MVP is
  complete.

## Inspiration

- GTA VI official visual identity.
- Rockstar Games editorial/product pages.
- The X-Files investigation atmosphere.
- Classic GTA community forums.
- GameVicio-style gaming community archives.
- Case boards, classified files and mystery documentation.

## Future Roadmap

1. MVP foundation: Home, core layout and design system primitives.
2. Cases archive: case cards, details, statuses and evidence structure.
3. Community layer: comments, submissions and moderation.
4. Authentication: accounts, profiles and contribution tracking.
5. Interactive map: location-based mysteries across Leonida.
6. Launch version: polished public experience with stable content workflows.
7. Future versions: advanced search, filters, notifications and integrations.
