# Personas

## GTA VI Mystery Hunter

Profile:

- Deeply interested in hidden details, easter eggs and unsolved mysteries.
- Compares screenshots, trailers, rumors and community findings.

Goals:

- Find active mysteries quickly.
- Track evidence and status updates.
- Separate confirmed discoveries from speculation.

Pain Points:

- Information is scattered across forums, videos and social posts.
- The same theories are repeated without evidence.

Success Criteria:

- Can scan cases by status.
- Can follow a mystery from summary to evidence.

## Community Theorist

Profile:

- Enjoys building and debating theories with other fans.
- Wants to contribute ideas and interpretations.

Goals:

- Submit theories.
- Compare alternative explanations.
- Join discussions around active cases.

Pain Points:

- Theory quality varies widely.
- Discussions can become unstructured.

Success Criteria:

- Can see theory context and evidence.
- Can understand what is speculation versus confirmed.

## Casual Visitor

Profile:

- Arrives from search, social media or a shared link.
- Wants an entertaining overview without deep research effort.

Goals:

- Understand what Archives VI is.
- Browse interesting mysteries.
- Read simple summaries.

Pain Points:

- Too much technical or community-specific language can be intimidating.

Success Criteria:

- Home page explains the site quickly.
- Cards and badges make content easy to scan.

## Moderator

Profile:

- Helps keep community submissions organized and safe.
- Reviews cases, evidence and discussions.

Goals:

- Identify low-quality or duplicate submissions.
- Mark content status clearly.
- Keep discussions productive.

Pain Points:

- Unclear workflows create inconsistent moderation.
- Evidence may lack sources or context.

Success Criteria:

- Statuses and moderation states are clear.
- Submission workflow is documented before implementation.

## Recruiter Reviewing the Portfolio

Profile:

- Evaluates product thinking, engineering quality and project organization.
- May not be familiar with GTA VI community details.

Goals:

- Understand the project purpose quickly.
- See evidence of architecture decisions.
- Review Scrum and product management artifacts.
- Assess front-end craftsmanship.

Pain Points:

- Portfolio projects often lack documentation and delivery structure.

Success Criteria:

- Documentation explains vision, roadmap, decisions and backlog.
- Codebase shows reusable components and thoughtful architecture.
